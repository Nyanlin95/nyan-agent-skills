## Purpose

Drive one conversion action: sign up, buy, or request a demo. Remove elements that
do not move the visitor toward it.

Show transformation before features. Present the outcome and the better future
state the visitor wants.

## Content

Compress the whole pitch into the first screen: what it is, why it matters, and
what to do next. Use one idea, one headline, and one call to action.

Prefer headline forms in this order:

1. Transformation
2. Outcome
3. Benefit
4. Feature

Use imagery that shows the visitor's future outcome rather than isolated product
screenshots. Add contrast treatment behind overlaid text and never distort images.

Reserve the accent color for actions so the CTA remains the strongest element.

## Structure

Use one alignment axis per section. Group related elements tightly and separate
groups generously.

Alternate text-heavy and visual sections. Do not stack sections with the same
density. Dark sections can add credibility and depth; light sections can explain
details.

Never use body text below 14 px. Use clear jumps in type scale, keep centered text
to two or three lines, and keep body lines near 50–75 characters.

Build a narrative arc: promise, proof, action. Move from what the product is, to
why it works, to why it can be trusted, to the next action.

Define narrative and messaging before visual decisions. Let the content drive the
design.
