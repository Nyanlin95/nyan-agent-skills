Supports separating thought development from prose refinement and retaining author judgment. No access to private skill implementations is claimed.
- [AI Style Guides: How to Help AI Write Like You](https://every.to/guides/ai-style-guide): gives concrete voice rules, structures, examples, anti-patterns, and review criteria for two different publications. Supports specific guides grounded in examples. Column-specific arcs and engagement figures are not universal rules or promised results for Nyan.

## Local decisions

The skill and mode guide are newly written adaptations. They omit universal writing quotas, compulsory autobiographical openings, blanket hedge bans, automatic publication, database writes, and mandatory interviews before a clear edit. Nyan's current brief and approved client voice govern each piece. A personal voice guide still requires samples or an explicit interview; this skill does not pretend that work has happened.
