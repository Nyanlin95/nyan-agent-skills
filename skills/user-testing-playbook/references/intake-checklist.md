# Intake checklist (fill before any session)

## The app
- Name, URL or build, version, environment (local / staging / production). Prefer local or staging.
- One-line pitch, exactly as a new visitor would hear it.
- Who it is for (audiences), and who it is NOT for.
- Top 3 jobs it must do for people. Order by importance.
- Platforms: desktop browsers, phone browsers, installed app, languages, themes.

## Access and data
- Test accounts (roles: new, existing with data, admin, read-only). Where the credentials are stored (never paste secrets in the log).
- Test data, and how to reset it.
- Anything behind an invite, paywall, captcha or email code: how to get past it legitimately.

## Boundaries
- Safe to click / never click (delete, pay, send, publish, invite, sign out).
- Real services behind the app (payments, email, SMS, social posting): are they in test mode?
- Rate limits, quiet hours, who must not be disturbed.
- Out of scope areas.

## What the owner already knows
- Known bugs, known rough edges, recent changes, riskiest areas.
- Past complaints or support tickets, analytics drop-off points if available.

## What the owner wants to learn
- Questions (can people sign up alone? is the checkout clear? is it fun?).
- What a good result looks like and who receives the report.

## Stop and ask when
- The app is live with real data or money and no test mode exists.
- Credentials or personal data are needed that were not provided.
- The app appears to block automation on purpose (do not work around it).
